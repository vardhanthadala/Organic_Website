Transparency first

Common Questions

Have questions about our sourcing, standards, or delivery? Here is how we do things.

How do you verify your farms are 100% chemical-free?

We regularly conduct on-site farm visits and test soil, water, and harvested produce batches for pesticide and chemical residues before packaging.

Why are your products processed in small batches?

Small-batch composting and sun-drying prevents nutrient loss, ensuring beneficial microbes and natural organic matter remain intact.

How long do your natural products stay fresh?

Because we do not add artificial stabilizers, we recommend storing our cow dung manures in a cool, dry place and using liquid tonics within 6–12 months.

Can I trace which gaushala or farm produced my order?

Yes! Every bag and bottle displays the harvest region and gaushala partner so you can trace your soil nutrition back to its origins.

Is your aged cow dung manure odor-free and safe for terrace gardens?

Yes! Because our cow dung is thoroughly sun-dried, composted, and finely sieved, it has a clean, earthy forest-floor scent and is completely safe for indoor pots, balconies, and terrace beds.
